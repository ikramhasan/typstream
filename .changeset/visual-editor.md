The visual editor (#36), beta: a Typst document edited on its own pages, so what the teacher edits is what prints and exports. New packages: `@typstream/syntax` (Typst's parser as wasm: the lossless syntax tree with UTF-16 offsets), `@typstream/editor-core` (the editor without a framework: `createEditor` puts a caret, a selection and the keyboard on the page view's pages; commands turn typing, Backspace, Enter, marks, block types, lists, alignment, tables and components into minimal edits of the source, with a word processor's list keys (Enter on an empty item moves it up a level or ends the list, Tab and Shift+Tab, Backspace at an item's start) and Ctrl+Enter out of a component (`exitBlock`); the caret stands where Typst lays out nothing (a paragraph's last space, an empty paragraph, item or cell: `caretAnchor`, a placeholder compiled there and never written to the source, `laidOut`); plugins after Plate's and ProseMirror's: keymaps with priorities that fall through, `handleKeyDown`, `handleTextInput`, `handlePaste`, decorations and plugin views, the editor's own keys a plugin (`keysPlugin`, `baseKeymap`: `deleteWord`, Tab through table cells with `moveCell`); `runKey`, PageUp/PageDown, Cut/Copy/Paste from the context menu, scrolling while dragging a selection, a `placeholder` in an empty document); empty lines written as a lone `~` (`EMPTY_LINE`, `isEmptyLine`) so the pages, the PDF and Word show them, and table cells that are a line high; components a package draws (a number line, answer lines) found on the pages through markers compiled around them, so they can be clicked and set up; `deleteBlock`; `EditorParamSpec.maxPositional` and `@typstream/editor-react` (`<TypstEditor>`: the fixed and floating toolbars, insert and slash menus, bars over components with their settings, table rows and columns, an equation's formula). The engine reports where the source was laid out on every page (`positions: true` on a document or compile; `PagedArtifact.positions`, `PagePositions`, `PageElement`, `DocumentState.artifactInput`, `setPositions`). A plugin's `editor` facet declares its components (`EditorComponentSpec`, `EditorParamSpec`); edu's (`eduComponents`) and mindmap's fill it in.

A font picker: a plugin's `editor.fonts` (`EditorFontsSpec`: the families it lists, a catalogue's index for its search, names people type and the family that stands in for each) puts its fonts in `<TypstEditor>`'s toolbar beside Typst's own (`BASE_FONTS`), and `setFont` and `fontAt` write and read the font as the size and colour are, in one `#text(…)`. `@typstream/google-fonts` fills the facet in: `GOOGLE_FONTS_FAMILIES` (36 families listed before a search), every family of the library by search, and `GOOGLE_FONTS_ALIASES` (Arial finds Arimo); the engine loads a family when the document names it.

After a pass over the whole editor as a teacher uses it: the clipboard has two sides (plain text as the selection shows it, rows and items on lines, cells apart by tabs; Typst for a paste in the editor, so marks, tables and components survive, a mark cut through closed); a block inserted or pasted in the middle of a paragraph splits it there; Backspace or Delete beside a component, an equation or a page break selects it first; a line break is one piece for the keys; a multiple choice's correct option follows it as options are added, moved and removed (`EditorComponentSpec.list` with a `maxPositional` parameter), and a component whose content is an outline keeps its shape (`EditorComponentSpec.outline`: mindmap's); marks change in place (a value changed isn't nested in the old one, sub- and superscript replace each other, a mark taken off part of a nested one leaves the others), `_` and `*` between letters are written as `#emph[…]` and `#strong[…]`, and `setLink` refuses `javascript:`, `data:` and `vbscript:` targets; clicks on a page break's room, beside and below equations, tables and components, and on components that draw no text put the caret where a word processor would; `matchesShortcut` (a key name against an event), `caretPlace` (the place a caret takes at an offset); in `<TypstEditor>`, the floating toolbar's link field, menus and toolbars by keys (Alt+F10, arrow keys, Escape back to the selection), Ctrl/Cmd+K, the slash menu at the caret, and a toolbar that scrolls sideways in a narrow pane.

And after a third pass, component by component: a form refuses what its component can't take and puts back what was there (a count out of its range, a size that isn't one), and a parameter named by digits is a positional argument (`EditorParamSpec.name`: `"0"` is the call's first); the equation's field keeps the last formula that compiled on the page while one is half typed, writes what math doesn't know as a teacher means it (a word as text: `speed`, the `km` of `3km`, the `CO` of `CO_2`; the letters a formula multiplies apart: `y = 2mx + b`, `E = mc^2`, `v = u + at`), and says why it refuses what it can't write; a component's bar sits above it, or below where the page's top is in the way; toolbar items whose command would change nothing are disabled (Redo, Indent and Outdent, Align in a list's item), and a press on the toolbars never takes the focus from the document; block types on an empty heading or item, and on one nested item, change that line only; a link is underlined with dots in the editor (`--typstream-link`; Typst gives it no look), its button drawn as on, and Ctrl/Cmd+K opens with the link's address; a new multiple choice or rubric has the caret in its first option or cell, the sample selected; more names find an item in the insert and slash menus.

What a component's package draws is edited where it is: a click on a formula or a text among a multiple choice's options written as code (`mcq(ce("H2O"), "Water")`) writes them as content (`argAsContent`, `argsAsContent`: `[#ce("H2O")]`, `[Water]`, which show the same) and selects the formula or puts the caret in the text, as one undo step; a click elsewhere on the component leaves the source as it was and selects it. A rubric's header is edited in its form: `levels` and `points` are `list` parameters (`EditorParamSpec.type: "list"`, `item`, `columnsFrom`: the criteria gain and lose cells with the levels), levels the document writes are text on the page, and `EditorComponentSpec.rows` gives the criteria a table's keys (Tab past the last cell adds a row, Backspace in an empty row removes it). A component's form follows it as it grows; a new argument of a call written an argument a line gets a line of its own. edu's `rubric` shows a level past the last of its points by its name alone.
