Streaming edits: a model changes part of a finished document, and the change streams into place. The model writes `<edit><old>…</old><new>…</new></edit>`s that quote the text they replace (`editPrompt()` after your system prompt, `editRequest(document, request)` as the user turn). `createEditSession(document)` applies an answer's edits as it streams (`update(textOf(message), done)`): a quote must match one place, exactly or with whitespace evened out, or the edit fails and is reported (`editFeedback` words it for the model). A quote of whole blocks streams its new text into a streaming range, so only that part heals as it's written and the rest stays; a quote inside a block is replaced once its new text is complete. While a quote streams, the text it matches is reported so the app can highlight it. A stopped answer rolls back the edit it was writing, the teacher's own edits (`userEdit`) move the answer's, and `keep()` / `undo()` end it. Selection edits: `blockRange(document, highlight)`, `selectionRequest`, and `session.write(range, replacement, done)`. Also `parseEdits`, `findQuote`, and `blockStarts` (from `@typstream/heal`). The package now depends on `@typstream/core` and `@typstream/heal`.
